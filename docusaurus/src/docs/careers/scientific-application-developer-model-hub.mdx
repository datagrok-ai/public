Location: Remote | Start: ASAP

You will own the development of a capability for scientific models - a custom
application we are building on the Datagrok platform for one of our pharma
customers. It's a central place where scientists register, discover, run, and
compare computational models. Models come from different teams, in different
languages, with different inputs and outputs. Your job is to turn that into one
coherent, fast, self-service experience.

This is not a ticket-driven role. You will "sit" with the customer's scientists,
understand how they work, and translate that into working software.


## Responsibilities

* Develop clean, high-performance, well-documented, production-level code
* Own your backlog: prioritize, estimate, communicate status, and drive features from a conversation to production
* Talk directly to the scientists and computational modelers: run demos, gather requirements, challenge assumptions, propose better ways to solve their problems on the platform
* Contribute improvements back to the platform and public plugins where the project uncovers gaps


## Requirements

* Expert-level TypeScript: you've designed and shipped non-trivial TS applications or libraries in pre-LLM era
* Engineering range: building elegant solutions from scratch, evolving complex existing codebases, and contributing efficiently across the whole stack, UI to backend
* Past projects in scientific or life-sciences software, complex modeling, or scientific computing (e.g., simulation engines, computational tools, statistical modeling apps, numerical/HPC code, etc.)
* Fluent spoken and written English: you will be in customer-facing meetings weekly, presenting your work and negotiating scope with scientists
* Willingness to learn new scientific domains
* Demonstrated ability to self-manage: you've owned a project or major workstream end-to-end and can point to something that shipped because you drove it to completion

## Nice to have

* Scientific background (STEM / life sciences)
* Python, R, C++, or Julia (enough to read and integrate scientists' model code)
* Experience with model lifecycle tooling (model registries, MLflow-style tracking, workflow engines)

## Why join us?

* Minimal bureaucracy, maximum impact
* Freedom to organize your work
* Challenging problems with real-world implications
* Collaboration with experts across multiple disciplines
* Continuous learning in cutting-edge tech and science
* Contributions to open source and democratization of data science
