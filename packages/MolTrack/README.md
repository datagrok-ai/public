# MolTrack

MolTrack registers chemical compounds, their batches and assay data, and identifies every compound
from its structure before it is saved.

With MolTrack you can:

* Register compounds and batches. A duplicate, or a salt of a registered compound, is shown before
  you save.
* Register compounds in bulk from a table and get their Grok IDs written back to it.
* Search compounds by structure (substructure, superstructure, similarity, exact) and by any field.
* Browse assays, their runs and results.
* Open a compound from its Grok ID in any table.

Open it from **Browse > Apps > Chem > MolTrack**.
