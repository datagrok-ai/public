# Peptides SAR — what the features do not claim

## Won't do

- Peptide Generation: the predictions, the basis columns and their cells, Novel (Exclude existing) and the generated
  table's columns are the package's 'Viewers: Peptide Generation' tests and the cell renderer's; the Add Viewer
  gallery and the Context Panel are the platform's routes to the same properties.
- Peptide Generation's default Activity column (it takes ID on the peptides dataset) and its use of the filter: no
  reading tells them apart; a product question, not a test gap.
