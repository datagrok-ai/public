The full ADMET panel has been calculated and appended to your table. The new columns cover all five categories:

- **Absorption** — Caco-2 permeability, solubility, bioavailability, etc.
- **Distribution** — VDss, plasma protein binding, BBB penetration, etc.
- **Metabolism** — CYP inhibition/substrate predictions (CYP1A2, CYP2C9, CYP2C19, CYP2D6, CYP3A4, etc.)
- **Excretion** — Half-life, clearance
- **Toxicity** — hERG inhibition, hepatotoxicity, AMES mutagenicity, etc.

Scroll right in the grid to see all the newly added columns. You can click any column header to sort, or use **Chem → Admetica → Calculate** from the top menu to re-run with a custom model selection.
