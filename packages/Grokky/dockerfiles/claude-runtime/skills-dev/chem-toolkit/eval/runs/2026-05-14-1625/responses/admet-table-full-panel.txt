The full ADMET panel has been calculated and appended to your table. The new columns cover all available endpoints across:

- **Absorption** – e.g. Caco-2 permeability, human intestinal absorption, bioavailability
- **Distribution** – e.g. BBB penetration, plasma protein binding, volume of distribution
- **Metabolism** – e.g. CYP enzyme inhibition/substrate predictions
- **Excretion** – e.g. half-life, clearance
- **Toxicity** – e.g. hERG inhibition, AMES mutagenicity, hepatotoxicity
