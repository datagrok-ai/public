The **Hit Triage** app is now open! 🎯

This workflow allows you to:
- **Ingest molecules** from various data sources (including ChEMBL or your own datasets)
- **Score and triage hits** through a multi-step campaign pipeline
- **Collaborate** by saving and sharing campaigns with your team

You can load demo data to get started quickly, or import your own compound library to begin triaging hits.
