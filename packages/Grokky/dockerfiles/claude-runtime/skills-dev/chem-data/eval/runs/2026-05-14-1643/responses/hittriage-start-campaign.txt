The **Hit Triage** app is now open! This workflow allows you to:

- **Ingest** a compound dataset (use demo data or upload your own)
- **Triage** hits by scoring and filtering molecules
- **Review** and annotate candidates
- **Submit** selected hits for further progression

You can load demo data (100 or 5000 molecules) from the data source options, or bring in your own compound list with a structure column.
