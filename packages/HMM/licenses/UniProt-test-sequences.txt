UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot sequences in test fixtures
===============================================

Unmodified UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot entries appear in the Datagrok HMM package's
test fixtures (files/tests):

- five entries (P01009, P02144, P02647, P68871, P69905) in search-* and scan-*
  (release 2026_03);
- 166 reviewed human entries carrying families of the built-in Pfam library, in
  clans-*, phmmer-* (sequence names sp_<accession>_<entry name>), retrieved
  from the UniProt REST API on 2026-10-11.
