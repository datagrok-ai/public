IMGT® data — attribution and license notice
==========================================

The ANARCI germline models and tables distributed with the Datagrok HMM
package (wasm/anarci/ALL.hmm.h3m.gz, germlines-*.json, species.json,
hmm-lengths.json) are derived from IMGT/GENE-DB germline V and J gene
sequences.

Source:       IMGT®, the international ImMunoGeneTics information system®,
              https://www.imgt.org (IMGT/GENE-DB, https://www.imgt.org/genedb/)

              ("The IMGT® data are provided to both public and private users
              under the CC BY-4.0 license", https://www.imgt.org/about/termsofuse.php,
              in effect since 1 July 2026.)
Changes made: the gapped V-gene and J-gene amino acid sequences were extracted
              and assembled into IMGT-numbered alignments per species and chain
              by ANARCI's build pipeline (https://github.com/oxpig/ANARCI,
              commit 79f6c575056dedef86cb8f405ebb039197923eec; J genes aligned
              with MUSCLE), converted into profile hidden Markov models with
              HMMER 3.4 hmmbuild --hand and hmmpress, and stored as JSON
              lookup tables.
Citation:     Giudicelli V., Chaume D. and Lefranc M.-P. IMGT/GENE-DB: a
              comprehensive database for human and mouse immunoglobulin and T
              cell receptor genes. Nucleic Acids Res. 33(suppl_1):D256-D261 (2005).
